# Practice sandbox 2

%%A second unpublished practice piece, so the walkthrough has another article to open. Its characters and events are invented.%%

The next morning the river was higher. Beaver checked the desk first and found it dry. Skunk checked the notebook and found a question written in the margin that neither of them remembered writing.

## The question in the margin

It asked what the desk was for. Skunk said it was for thinking. Beaver said it was for finishing. They agreed to keep the question and answer it later.

- Read the question aloud.
- Write one answer each.
- Keep the better one.
