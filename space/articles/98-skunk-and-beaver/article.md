# Practice sandbox

%%An unpublished fictional practice piece. Its characters and claims are invented for the walkthrough.%%

Skunk brought a notebook to the river. Beaver brought a branch. They had agreed to build a place to think, but neither had asked what the other meant by a place. 





## A desk by the water

“We need a flat surface,” said Skunk. “We need it to stay dry,” said Beaver. Both wrote that down.[^1]  

This paragraph is here for your first **comment**. Choose a few words, leave a thought, and follow the quote back to its place.

### Make room for a thought

Skunk moved the first paragraph below the second. The story made more sense. The words had stayed the same; their order had changed what a reader understood.

###### The smallest mark

## What belongs on the desk

- A notebook for the first rough thought.
- A source passage for checking it.
    - Keep its original words in view.
    - Keep the link that lets you return.
- [ ] Read the whole paragraph before changing it.
- [x] Leave space for a different answer.

1. Write the problem in your own words.
2. Ask which observation would change your mind.
3. Keep the useful part and try again.
